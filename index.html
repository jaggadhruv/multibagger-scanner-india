Method

Every check is a fixed yes or no. A check with missing data counts as not met. Thresholds live in config.py.

Gate 1 · Proven business

  • 1. Revenue growth of at least 15% a year over 3 years
  • 2. Revenue higher in each of the last 3 years
  • 3. Return on capital employed of at least 20%, averaged over 3 years
  • 4. Operating cash flow positive in at least 3 of the last 4 years
  • 5. Operating cash flow of at least 70% of net profit over 3 years
  • 6. Borrowings of no more than 0.6 times shareholders' funds
  • 7. Promoters hold at least 30%, have not sold more than 3 points over a year, and no pledge above 5% is flagged on screener.in

Gate 2 · Early / inflecting

  • 8. Operating margin at least 1 percentage point(s) above 3 years ago, and not down more than 1.5 on last year
  • 9. Latest-year growth of at least 12% and at least 60% of the 3-year pace; latest quarter up at least 8% on a year ago when that figure is available

Gate 3 · Chart strength

  • 10. Weekly Supertrend (ATR 10, multiplier 2.5) on BUY
  • 11. Emerging (BUY for 12 weeks or less): price above the 200-day average. Established: price above a rising 200-day average and within 15% of the 52-week high
  • 12. Emerging: ahead of the Nifty 50 over 3 months. Established: ahead over both 6 and 12 months

Not investment advice. This is a screening tool for research. The checklist has not been validated against past returns; the track record above is the only evidence it will ever produce, and it needs months to mean anything.
